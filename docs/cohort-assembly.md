# Primary cohort assembly

The primary cohort contains one adjudicated adult IHCA case and one control per risk set. Run `ihca-experiments assemble` separately for the development and external site. Times may use ISO 8601 offsets. Supply all offset-free times in UTC, or convert local wall time to UTC before export. Encounter, patient and recording identifiers are strings.

| Export | Required fields | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Encounters | `encounter_id`, `patient_id`, `age`, `sex`, `admission_time`, `discharge_time`, `first_ihca_time`, `case_eligible` | One row per encounter. Age is in years, sex uses the [model convention](data.md). Use an empty arrest time if no arrest occurred. A first arrest, including one ineligible for case selection, ends eligibility as a control. Use 1 only for an adjudicated eligible first arrest, otherwise 0. Optional `rhythm` and `etiology` annotate cases for subgroup analysis. |
| Departments | `encounter_id`, `department`, `start_time`, `end_time` | One dated interval per ward stay. Use the same department vocabulary throughout a site. Intervals must fit inside the admission and must not overlap. |
| ECGs | `encounter_id`, `record_id`, `ecg_time`, `clinical_time`, `waveform_path`, `sampling_rate_hz`, `quality_score`, model features | One row per recording linked to its snapshot of clinical measurements. The encounter supplies age and sex. Provide the other 19 clinical variables and the 13 automated ECG variables defined in the [data contract](data.md). XML may supply the automated variables. Optional comparator variables follow the [experiment protocol](experiments.md). Waveform paths resolve relative to the ECG export. |

Use the same `--xml-map` with `assemble` and `ihca prepare` for vendor-specific XML. Eligibility requires the full predictor set, acceptable quality, calibration and an adult patient. Clinical inputs must already be available when the ECG is recorded. Files referenced by otherwise eligible exports must be accessible. Cases without an eligible pre-arrest ECG are counted in the command summary. Fix unknown encounters, ambiguous ward histories and missing index wards in the exports.

Ward intervals include their start and exclude their end. A transfer at the index instant belongs to the arriving ward. If a case arrest coincides with discharge, the final ward that ends at discharge is used. A potential control must still be hospitalized and free of any first arrest strictly beyond its assigned index. Gaps in transfer history make a candidate ineligible at those times. The department at ECG acquisition may differ from the index department.

The case index is its first arrest. Every candidate is assessed at its own admission plus the case's elapsed time to arrest. Among other patients with matching sex and index department and a complete eligible ECG, choose the closest age. Equal distances resolve by ascending encounter ID. Reuse across risk sets is allowed. For simultaneous eligible ECGs the lexicographically largest recording ID wins. The output contains one case and one control per pair, unique observation IDs, source recording and encounter IDs, pair and patient IDs, assigned index times and predictor values. The training splitter groups repeat patients with their matched pairs. An otherwise eligible case without a match raises an error identifying its encounter.
